A cooperative mystery on paper, for two players

Two clue sheets, a conversation, and one answer. Start with a complete free case; no game app, account or card deck is required.

Set up the free case

  1. Save the five-page English PDF below. Print single-sided on A4 at actual size.
  2. Give Player A page 2 and Player B page 3. Keep the shared setup page nearby. Keep page 4 (hints) and page 5 (answer and ending) separate.
  3. Read your own clue and talk about it. Do not turn over the solution before choosing your answer. One person can read both clues for solo play.
  4. If you want help, open only your hint. Finish by checking the reason and reading the complete ending.
